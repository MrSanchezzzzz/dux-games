Privacy Policy for Afterburn

App: Afterburn · Platform: iOS · Effective date: November 18, 2026

Short version: You don't need an account. Your progress and settings are saved on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account — we run no servers and never receive them. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Ads are optional reward videos from Google AdMob that you choose to watch; where required, your consent is collected through Google's consent form and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can change it any time. We do not sell your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how DUX Games Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you play Afterburn (the “Game”) on iPhone and iPad.

By playing the Game, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Who we are

Afterburn is developed by DUX Games Studio, an independent solo developer. For any privacy questions, contact us at kremenchuksh@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or to provide your name, email, or address to play.

a) Information stored on your device and in your iCloud

Your game progress and settings (campaign progress, ships, upgrades, currencies, medals, purchases owned, preferences, and your advertising-consent choice) are stored locally on your device. If iCloud is enabled for the Game on your device, a copy is kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so your progress follows you to your other devices. We cannot access your iCloud data.

b) Information collected automatically by third-party services

When you buy something or watch a reward video, our providers may automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, operating system and version, language, and general region);
  • Device and app identifiers (such as the identifier for vendors and installation or session identifiers);
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt;
  • Purchase and transaction information (the product, its price, transaction details);
  • Ad interaction data (for example, that an ad was shown, completed, or tapped);
  • Your IP address, used to approximate your country and to deliver ads.

We do not collect precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

3. How we use information

  • To run the Game and keep your progress (including across your devices through iCloud);
  • To process and restore your purchases;
  • To show the reward videos you choose to watch and give you their reward;
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and duplicate transactions;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

The Game uses the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple — App Store purchases, iCloud, Game Center, the share sheet and ratings. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies) — connects the Game to the App Store for purchases; Unity receives purchase/transaction information, device and app identifiers, device information and approximate country to run the service and prevent duplicate transactions. See the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — shows the consent form and stores your advertising-consent choice.

Where applicable, the full list of advertising partners is shown inside the consent form and on Google's list of ad technology providers.

5. Advertising and your choices

The Game shows only optional rewarded ads: a short video you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus (for example, double Credits after a win, a refuelled tank, or a daily supply drop). There are no forced or interstitial ads, and none appear during play.

  • Personalised ads may use your advertising identifier and interaction data — only with your consent where it is required.
  • Non-personalised ads are based on general context, not on your past behaviour.

You can change your choices at any time:

  • In the Game: Settings → “AD PRIVACY” (shown where consent applies, e.g. in the EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, to allow or deny tracking for the Game.

iOS Tracking (ATT)

Before your IDFA can be used to measure or personalise advertising across other companies' apps and websites, we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (after a short explanation). If you decline, the IDFA is not used for tracking and ads are still available.

6. Purchases

Afterburn offers optional in-app purchases (for example, packs of Prisms, the Starter Pack and the Supporter Pack). All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store — we never receive, see, or store your payment card or billing details. Purchases you own (like the Supporter Pack) can be restored in Settings → “RESTORE”. Refunds are handled by Apple under its terms.

7. iCloud, Game Center, sharing and ratings

  • iCloud: as described above, your save is copied to your own iCloud account if iCloud is on; you can turn this off in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center, your medals are reported as achievements to Apple. Your Game Center profile is managed by Apple.
  • Sharing: when you tap SHARE, the Game creates a picture of your result and opens Apple's share sheet; nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it. Saving to Photos asks for your permission to add images only.
  • Ratings: the Game may occasionally show Apple's standard rating prompt; your rating goes to Apple.

8. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

  • Contract — to provide the Game and the purchases you make;
  • Consent — for personalised advertising (you may withdraw it at any time);
  • Legitimate interests — for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention, security, and keeping the Game working.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us at kremenchuksh@gmail.com and we will help remove it.

10. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with the providers listed above, strictly to run the Game, process purchases and serve the ads you choose to watch, and where required by law.

11. Data retention

We do not keep our own user database. Your save stays on your device (and in your iCloud, if enabled) until you delete the Game, clear its data, or remove it from iCloud. Our providers keep data according to their own policies.

12. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, using safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where the law requires.

13. Your choices & rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent.

  • EEA/UK: your rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, including withdrawing ad consent at any time via Settings → “AD PRIVACY”.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, including sharing for personalised ads. We do not sell personal information for money.

Because we hold no data on our own servers, deleting the Game removes its local data. For requests about data held by our providers, contact us and we will direct the request to the right provider. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

14. Security

We rely on reputable providers (Apple, Unity, Google) that apply industry-standard security measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

15. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept them.
