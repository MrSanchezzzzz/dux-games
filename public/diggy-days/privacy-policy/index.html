Privacy Policy for Diggy Days

App: Diggy Days · Platforms: Android & iOS · Effective date: August 12, 2026

Short version: Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device — we run no servers and require no account. We use anonymous Firebase Analytics and show rewarded ads via Google AdMob. Where required, your consent is managed through Google UMP, and you can change your choices any time in Settings. We do not sell your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how DUX Games Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you play Diggy Days (the “Game”) on Android and iOS.

By playing the Game, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Who we are

Diggy Days is developed by DUX Games Studio, an independent solo developer. We do not operate a physical storefront. For any privacy questions, contact us at kremenchuksh@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email, or address to play. We collect the following:

a) Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (inventory, upgrades, preferences, and your advertising-consent choice) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

b) Information collected automatically (via third-party services)

When you play, our third-party providers may automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, operating system and version, language, and general region);
  • A mobile advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, where permitted);
  • App usage and interaction data (e.g. sessions, in-game events, ad interactions);
  • IP address (used to approximate country/region and to deliver ads), which is not stored by us in an identifiable form.

We do not knowingly collect precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Game;
  • Understand how players use the Game through aggregated, anonymous analytics;
  • Display advertising (including rewarded ads you choose to watch);
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and technical problems;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

The Game uses the following services, which process data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — see Google's Privacy Policy and “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics) — see Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — manages your advertising consent choices.

A full list of advertising partners is shown to you within the consent form (where applicable) and at Google's partner list.

5. Advertising and your choices

The Game shows rewarded ads. Depending on your region and consent:

  • Personalised ads may use your advertising identifier and interaction data to show more relevant ads (only with your consent where required).
  • Non-personalised ads are shown based on general context, not your past behaviour.

You can control ads at any time:

  • In the Game: open Settings → “Manage Ad Consent” (shown where consent applies) to change your choice.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your Advertising ID / opt out of ad personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to disable app tracking.

You can also turn off anonymous analytics in Settings → “Share anonymous analytics” inside the Game.

6. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising and, where required, analytics (you may withdraw consent at any time);
  • Legitimate interests — for non-personalised ads, basic analytics, security, and improving the Game.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us at kremenchuksh@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with the third-party providers listed above, strictly to operate the Game and serve ads, and where required by law.

9. Data retention

We do not maintain our own user database. Third-party providers retain data according to their own policies. Locally stored game data remains on your device until you delete the Game or clear its data.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. These providers implement safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) as required by applicable law.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent.

  • EEA/UK: rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money.

To exercise any right, contact us at kremenchuksh@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

12. Security

We rely on reputable providers (Google) who apply industry-standard security measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top reflects the latest version. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance.
